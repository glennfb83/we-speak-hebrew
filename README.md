# we-speak-hebrew
arguably THE greatest game oat
A game called "We Speak Hebrew" where you are sent around israel on a quest to find the ancient holy land of "New Tel Aviv, Tuvalu" hidden in the depths, this is how you beat the game, but along the way you will encounter people who give you clues, some will tell you the truth, these are the "Hebrew Whisperers" while some try to deceive you and send you the wrong way, these people are called "Yiddish Manipulators"
